What is Skill Flex?+

Skill Flex is a student services marketplace. You can discover services, view student provider profiles, message providers, request bookings, and publish your own skills as services. You can also post a campus request when you need help finding the right service.

How do I find services?+

Create an account or log in, then explore Discover. Search for a service, browse categories, and filter by price, delivery type, campus verification, or published availability. Open a provider's profile to learn more, save a service, or start a conversation.

Can I offer my skills?+

Yes. After signing up and completing your profile, use the app's option to offer a service. Add a title, description, category, price, and delivery type. You can manage your listings and publish your availability in My services.

How do bookings work?+

Choose a service, agree on the scope and price, and send a booking request with your preferred date and details. Choose an available time when the provider has published a schedule. The provider confirms the request, and you can follow its progress in My bookings. Payments are arranged directly with the provider; Skill Flex does not process them.

How does student verification work?+

Email verification during sign-in is separate from campus verification. To request a campus badge, submit your university email and a clear photo of your current student ID in the app. An administrator reviews the submission privately. The badge appears only after approval, and the ID photo is deleted after review. A campus badge confirms reviewed student status; it does not guarantee the quality of a service.
