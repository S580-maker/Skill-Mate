Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using Skillmate.

How do I request a booking?

Open a provider’s listing, choose Request booking, then select a service, date and time. You can coordinate final details in Messages.

How do payments work?

Skillmate does not collect payment for bookings. Agree on the price and a safe payment method directly with the provider before the appointment.

How do I become a provider?

Open Profile, choose Offer a service, and add a clear description, price and examples of your work.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Open My bookings and message the other student as soon as possible to agree on a change or cancellation.

How does campus verification work?

Submit your university email and a clear photo of your current student ID. A campus badge appears only after review and approval. Your ID photo is private and deleted after review.

What should I do if someone behaves inappropriately?

Use Report on their profile or booking, block them if needed, and contact support. Do not share passwords or banking details.

How do I update my profile or password?

Open Profile & Settings from the sidebar to update your profile details, change your password and choose notification preferences.
